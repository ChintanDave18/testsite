Services and prices

Labor only. Parts are charged at cost and we tell you the number before we fit anything.

Tune-ups

$75

Safety check

Brakes, shifting, tire pressure, bolt torque, and a short road test. Good before a long ride or a trip.

$145

Full tune-up

Everything in the safety check plus drivetrain clean, wheel truing, cable adjustment, and bearing inspection.

$260

Overhaul

Stripped, cleaned, regreased and rebuilt. For bikes coming back after a few years in storage.

Individual repairs

$25

Flat repair

Tube swap or patch, valve check, and a look at what caused it so it does not happen again next week.

$40

Brake service

Pad replacement, cable or hose adjustment, rotor truing. Hydraulic bleeds are $65 per wheel.

$45

Gear adjustment

Derailleur alignment, cable tension, hanger straightening. Covers skipping, ghost shifting and dropped chains.

$60

Wheel truing

Lateral and radial truing with tension balanced across the spokes. Replacement spokes billed separately.

$55

Drivetrain swap

Chain, cassette and chainring replacement, with a wear check so you only change what is actually worn.

$90

New bike build

Box-to-road assembly for a bike bought online, including a follow-up adjustment two weeks later.

Travel and extras

Visits within 12 miles of downtown are included. Beyond that it is $1.50 per mile each way. Two or more bikes at one address take $15 off each repair, so it is worth rounding up the family bikes before you book.
