One van, one mechanic, no counter to wait at.

Ridgeline started in 2019 after a decade of shop work and one too many customers leaving a bike for nine days in July.

How we work

The van carries a full bench, a truing stand, a torque wrench for every fastener on a modern bike, and the parts that account for nine out of ten repairs. If a job needs something unusual, we order it and come back rather than leaving your bike in pieces.

You get a text when we are fifteen minutes out. Work happens in your driveway or the parking spot outside your building, and we clean up the chain grease before we leave.

What we will not do

We will not sell you a drivetrain you do not need. Chains get measured, not guessed at. If a repair costs more than the bike is worth, we will say so and tell you what a sensible replacement looks like, even though that is a sale we lose.

We also do not work on gas-powered conversions or e-bike battery internals. Motors and controllers, yes. Cells, no.

Where we go

Regular service area covers the north and west suburbs, with Saturday mornings usually spent closer to town. If you are unsure whether you are in range, send your address and we will answer straight.
