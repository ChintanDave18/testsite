A small shop that remembers your measurements.

Meher & Co. opened in 2011 with two racks and one sewing machine. The machine is still here.

Where the fabric comes from

Twice a year we buy in person — Kanchipuram and Varanasi for silk, Surat for georgette and organza, Jaipur for hand work on bridal sets. We buy from the same families each time, which is why a saree can be reordered in a second color rather than only admired.

Everything is labelled with what it actually is. Pure zari is sold as pure zari and tested zari is sold as tested zari, at the price each deserves.

The tailoring room

Blouses, alterations and bridal stitching happen upstairs, by two tailors who have been with the shop for over a decade. Nothing is shipped out, so a fitting can be corrected while you wait rather than next week.

What we will not do

We will not take a bridal order with less than six weeks on the clock. Rushed hand work looks rushed, and you will see it in the photographs for the rest of your life.

We also will not push you up a range. If the outfit you want exists at half the budget you came in with, we will show you that one first and say so plainly.

Come and feel the fabric

Photographs flatten silk. The shop is small, the tea is free, and nobody will follow you around the racks.
