Sarees

Everyday and office

Cotton, linen and chiffon in plain and small-print. Light enough to wear through a workday and wash at home.

Party and festival

Georgette, organza and soft silk with sequin, zari or thread work. The bulk of what people buy for sangeets and pujas.

$450–$2,200

Wedding silk

Kanjivaram, Banarasi and Paithani, bought directly from weaving families. Pure zari pieces sit at the top of this range.
