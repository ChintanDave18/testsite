What is on the racks

Sarees

Kanjivaram and Banarasi silk for weddings, plus georgette, chiffon and cotton for everything that is not a wedding. Every silk saree includes fall, pico and a stitched blouse.

Lehengas and gowns

Bridal sets, sangeet lehengas and lighter pieces for guests who still have to dance. Bridal orders need eight weeks; guest pieces need two.

Salwar suits

Anarkalis, straight-cut and palazzo sets for everyday wear, office festivals and mehndi functions. Stock sizes altered free within a week.

Girls

Small lehengas and pattu pavadai, sized so they survive one more growth spurt. Stitched in a week.

How a bridal order runs

  1. Come in with your dates and any photos you have saved. We pull fabric and talk budget honestly in the first hour.

  2. Measurements and a written estimate. Half now, half at collection, and nothing is cut until you approve the fabric in daylight.

  3. Two fittings, roughly four and seven weeks in. Small changes at the second fitting are included.

  4. Collection with a steam press, a garment bag, and a note of how to store it afterwards.

Wedding season books up early

For a spring wedding, come to us by January. Guest outfits and alterations we can usually turn around inside two weeks, but bridal work needs its eight.
