What we fix most

Three jobs make up most of our week. Everything else is on the services page.

Flats and tires

Punctures, worn treads, tubeless top-ups, and the slow leak you have been pumping up every morning for a month.

Brakes and gears

Cables, pads, rotors, and shifting that skips under load. We road-test every bike before we pack up.

Seasonal tune-ups

A full going-over after a winter in the garage: drivetrain clean, bearings checked, everything trued and torqued.

How a visit works

  1. Tell us the bike, the problem, and your address. Photos help but are not required.

  2. We reply the same day with a price range and two or three time slots.

  3. The van shows up, the work happens, and you pay once you have ridden it.
